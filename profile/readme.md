> [!IMPORTANT]
> Survival Log is a single-player survival game. All options are **client-side only**.

## What it does

Survival Log is a text-based survival RPG where every decision matters — manage hunger, thirst, health, and resources while exploring a dangerous wilderness. One bad choice ends your run.

This trainer removes the risk. **Infinite health** keeps you alive, **no hunger or thirst** eliminates the survival clock, and **unlimited resources** lets you craft everything. **Unlock all blueprints** gives you full crafting access from the start.

## Features

| Option | Hotkey | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Infinite health | `F1` | Client |
| No hunger depletion | `F2` | Client |
| No thirst depletion | `F3` | Client |
| Unlimited inventory space | `F4` | Client |
| Max resources on gather | `F5` | Client |
| Resource multiplier | slider | Client, `1x`–`20x` |
| Instant crafting | — | Client |
| Unlock all blueprints / recipes | — | Persistent |
| Unlock all locations | — | Persistent |
| Movement speed | slider | Client, `1x`–`5x` |
| Free camera | `F10` | Client |
| Hide HUD | `F11` | Client |

## Hotkeys

`Insert` opens the menu · `End` resets everything · `F1`–`F11` as above, all rebindable

> [!TIP]
> **No hunger/thirst** + **unlimited resources** turns Survival Log from a survival game into a pure exploration and story experience — enjoy the narrative without the pressure.

> [!WARNING]
> **Resource multiplier above 10x** can break the game's economy. Use gradually.
